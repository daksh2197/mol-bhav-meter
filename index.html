The math

        

          walk-away cost = your price ÷ (1 + sales tax) × (1 − profit target) − fees − shipping − packing
        

        

          That is the most one product can cost you and still leave the profit you asked for. A great deal is a
          cost at or under 75% of it. The meter then adds tax and a share of delivery
          to the vendor's price and sees which side of each line it lands on.
        

        

          Everything runs on your phone. There is no server and no account, and once the page has loaded it works
          with no signal. A link you share carries the numbers inside the link itself.
        

        

          This is a calculator, not tax advice. You type the rates, so check them for your product and your
          country.
